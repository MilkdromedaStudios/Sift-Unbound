# Armor & Gear — Dungeons II-inspired fan implementation

[Home](Home.md) · [Items](Items.md) · [The Sift research](The-Sift-Research.md)

> **Not a vanilla 2027 feature announcement.** These 12 wearable items are an original Minecraft Java survival adaptation of armor *names* from Minecraft Dungeons II. Dungeons II uses action-RPG armor effects; the protection points, durability, textures, repair ingredients and recipes below are mod-authored. The official Java/Bedrock Sift has **no confirmed armor sets, recipes or stats** yet.

## Armor sets (helmet / chest / leggings / boots)

| Set | In-game names | Defense points | Base durability | Toughness | Repair item | Main crafting item |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Sifter** | Hood, Robe, Leggings, Boots | 2 / 5 / 4 / 1 | 16 | 0 | Teal Sprouts | Teal Sprouts |
| **Humbler** | Antenna, Carapace, Greaves, Tarsi | 3 / 6 / 5 / 2 | 24 | 1 | Husk Shell | Husk Shell |
| **Monarch** | Crown, Mantle, Sash, Talons | 3 / 8 / 6 / 3 | 33 | 2 | Monarch Feather | Monarch Feather |

The Minecraft durability formula varies by equipment slot. The Monarch set also has 0.1 knockback resistance from the armor material. These armor sets grant their listed Minecraft defense stats; *Dungeons II* special effects (such as a Crown loot bonus or Sifter debuffs) are **not implemented** and should not be advertised as working.

## Crafting

Use the regular helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots armor shapes in a crafting table:

* **Sifter**: use **Teal Sprouts** as the armor material. For the **Robe**, place a **Soul Shard** in the center.
* **Humbler**: use **Husk Shell** blocks. For the **Carapace**, place a **Soul Shard** in the center.
* **Monarch**: use **Monarch Feathers** dropped by the Monarch boss. For the **Mantle**, place an **Echo Shard** in the center.

All three sets can be enchanted and repaired in an anvil with their matching repair ingredient. They are also visible in the **The Sift Unbound** creative tab.

## Visuals and future parity

Each set includes four original inventory icons and a matching original colored armor texture (including the separate baby-humanoid layer required by 26.3). The textures are initial pixel-art concepts, not reconstructed or copied Mojang assets.

The official upcoming vanilla Sift has **not** confirmed that any of these sets or crafting systems will be added. If Mojang publishes different mechanics, this mod can update them without deleting established worlds or changing old item IDs.
