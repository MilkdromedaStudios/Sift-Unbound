### Echo Tuner

<img src="../src/main/resources/assets/the_rift/textures/item/echo_tuner.png" width="64" height="64" alt="Echo Tuner texture">

Rare. Use it on - or inside - a reinforced-deepslate Warden Portal frame (the one in the middle of every ancient city) to play the melody that awakens it. One use per awakening. Also found in ~12% of ancient city chests.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | `the_rift:echo_tuner` |
| Type | Tool (16 uses) |

- Crafting table: `·E·`<br>`EAE`<br>`·N·`<br>`E` = Echo Shard, `A` = Amethyst Shard, `N` = Note Block -> **1x Echo Tuner**
