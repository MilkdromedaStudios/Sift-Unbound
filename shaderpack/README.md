# Sift Unbound Shaders

An original Iris shader pack made for **The Sift Unbound**, and at home in every other dimension too.

* **Shadows** from the sun and moon, and in the Sift from the tide light, with soft edges.
* **Sift tide light**: teal at the Flow Tide, deep blue at the Endure Tide, salmon-gold at the Thrive Tide.
* **Blocky clouds**: the game's fancy 3D clouds, lit from above with shaded sides and dark undersides.
* **Details that pop**: filmic tonemapping, sharpening, vibrance and contrast, a gentle vignette.
* Waving grass, flowers and leaves (Sift plants included), torchlight warmth, sun glints on water and ichor.
* **Overworld, Nether, End and the Sift** each have their own lighting; dimensions from other mods use the Overworld's.
* Quality profiles: **Potato**, Low, Medium, High, Ultra, plus every option on its own.

Needs **Iris** (with Sodium). Put the zip in `.minecraft/shaderpacks` and pick it in Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs.
No OptiFine support.

All code is original, written for this project. Part of [The Sift Unbound](https://github.com/MilkdromedaStudios/Sift-Unbound).
